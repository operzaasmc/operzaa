Privacy Policy

Last updated September 2026. Plain-language version: we collect only what you send us, and we don't sell it.

What we collect

Only what you choose to send us.

This site does not use cookies or tracking scripts, and it doesn't run analytics on visitors. The only information we receive is what you send us directly — for example, your name, email address, company and message when you contact us to book a strategy call.

How we use it

To respond to you, and to deliver the engagement.

We use contact information to reply to your inquiry and, if you become a client, to deliver and manage the engagement. We don't sell your information, and we don't share it with third parties for their own marketing.

During an engagement

We work inside your tools — not a separate system.

As described elsewhere on this site, an active engagement's team works inside your own CRM and helpdesk rather than migrating your data into a system we control. Confidentiality and data-handling terms specific to your business are set out in the signed engagement agreement, which governs alongside this policy.

Your rights

Ask us to access, correct or delete it, any time.

Email hello@operzaa.com and we'll act on it directly. This policy may be updated as the company grows; material changes will be reflected here with a new "last updated" date.
