How it works

From diagnostic to running operation.

No blind annual commitment. Every engagement starts with a scoped diagnostic, not a long-term contract signed on faith. Here's exactly what happens at each stage.

01

Diagnose

A strategy call maps your current operation — volume, channels, tooling, and where it's breaking. No pitch deck, no pressure to sign anything.

What we ask about

Current headcount and coverage, ticket or lead volume, the tools already in place, and what "working" would actually look like for your team.

What you walk away with

A clear picture of what a managed function would need to cover — whether or not you move forward.

02

Design the operating model

We scope the function, the AI workflows underneath it, and the reporting you'll see — specific to your business, not a template pulled off a shelf.

What's produced

A written scope: defined coverage, response targets, escalation paths, and the metrics we'll report on.

Your input

Sign-off on scope, and access to the tools the team will work inside — nothing more.

03

Deploy the team & workflows

A dedicated team is built and trained inside your existing tools; automation is configured and tested before it ever touches a real customer.

Before going live

A shadow period against real, anonymized volume — reviewed internally before customer-facing work begins.

Tooling

The team works inside your CRM and helpdesk. Nothing is migrated to a system you can't see into.

04

Manage, report, iterate

You get a named point of contact and a standing reporting cadence — reviewing outcomes, not managing individuals day to day.

Reporting cadence

A regular review against the metrics agreed in scope, plus visibility into volume and quality whenever you want it.

Changing scope

Coverage and priorities shift as your business does — that's a conversation with your point of contact, not a change-request form.

What a diagnostic call covers

One conversation, four questions.

01Where is the operation breaking right now — sales, support, success, or all three?
02What does current volume and coverage actually look like?
03What tools are already in place, and what needs to stay untouched?
04What would "working" look like in 90 days?
