FAQ

Straight answers before you book a call.

Why isn't pricing listed on this site?+

Engagements are scoped to the specific function, volume and coverage you need — a fixed public price would either be misleading for most companies or would force everyone into a package that doesn't fit. A strategy call gives you a real, specific number in one conversation.

Do we pay before we see any results?+

No. Every new engagement opens with a free first week — we run the function inside your own tools and you see real output before any invoice goes out. If it isn't working, you walk away having paid nothing.

How is this different from an outsourcing company?+

An outsourcing vendor typically sells seats or hours and leaves quality control and reporting to you. OPERZAA takes ownership of the function itself — scope, process, QA and reporting included — so you review outcomes, not individual contractor timesheets.

What happens to our data and customer relationships?+

The team works inside your own CRM and helpdesk rather than migrating your data into a separate system we control. Confidentiality is part of every engagement agreement, and our security practices continue to formalize as the company grows.

How much oversight will this take from me?+

You get a single named point of contact and a standing reporting cadence. You're reviewing outcomes on a schedule you set, not managing day-to-day work or approving every ticket.

How fast can we start?+

Most engagements move from strategy call to a scoped plan within days, and from scoped plan to a live operation within a few weeks — depending on how much integration your tools require.

Can we change scope, or exit if it isn't working?+

Engagements are structured to earn renewal, not lock you into a term regardless of fit. Scope can be adjusted as your needs change — that's discussed explicitly on the strategy call.
